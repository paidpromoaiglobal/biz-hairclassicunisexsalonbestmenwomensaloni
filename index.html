About Us

Hair Classic Unisex Salon: Best men & women salon in Durgapura, Tonk Road, Jaipur

Hair Classic Unisex Salon is Durgapura's go-to destination for men and women who refuse to compromise on style or quality. Nestled in Kedawat Bhawan on Tonk Road, we have built a reputation for precision haircuts, expert grooming, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere that keeps our guests coming back — some since as early as 2023. With a stellar 4.9-star rating across 238 reviews, we are proud to be one of Jaipur's most loved salons.

Our skilled and friendly team brings passion and professionalism to every chair, ensuring that whether you are stepping in for a quick trim or a complete makeover, you leave looking and feeling your absolute best. We are open every day from 8:30 AM to 10:00 PM, so great hair is always just around the corner. Affordable rates, a clean environment, and that perfect 'paisa vasool' experience — that is the Hair Classic promise.
